3. High-Speed Layer Allocation, Layer Transitions & Via Matching

Traces routed on Layer 1 (Outer Microstrip) require different widths than Layer 3 (Inner Stripline). When routing from one layer to another, return-current ground vias and antipad tuning prevent high-frequency reflections.

Inter-Layer Signal Transition & Return-Path Ground Via Design

IPC-2141A §3.3 / High-Speed Layout Standard

When differential pairs jump from an outer layer (e.g. L1 Microstrip) to an inner layer (e.g. L3 Stripline), the via barrel introduces parasitic capacitance ($C_{\text{via}}$) and inductance ($L_{\text{via}}$). To maintain continuous impedance and prevent ground bounce, place a GND Stitching Via immediately alongside the signal transition.
